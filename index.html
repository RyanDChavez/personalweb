About

  1. Idi Amin’s Uganda (Princeton · 2015–2019). I went to Princeton to study history and ended up focused on East Africa: I studied Swahili and earned a certificate in African Studies. My senior thesis, “Idi Amin’s Uganda and Tanzanian Foreign Policy 1971–1972: From Mwongozo wa TANU to The Mogadishu Agreement,” won the prize for best thesis in African History.
  2. City in the Clouds (College · 2015–2019). Alongside school I played bass in a band, City in the Clouds. We toured a couple of times and played around SXSW in 2019. Our album, Lost in the City, is out on Spotify.
  3. Rockies (Kampala, Uganda · Princeton in Africa). After graduating I moved to Kampala on a Princeton in Africa fellowship, in the country my thesis was about. I worked at Rockies, a small NGO focused on youth arts and entertainment with a social enterprise side, across communications and monitoring and evaluation.
  4. Nearside (San Francisco · 2020–2023). In 2020 I joined Nearside, a business neobank and lending platform, as employee 18. I started in operations, running identity checks for new customers, then moved into product to work on debit cards and business lines of credit. I stayed through the acquisition.
  5. Stanford Law (After Nearside). When Nearside wrapped up, I applied to law school and got into Stanford Law. Then I decided I didn’t want to be a lawyer.
  6. Teaching (Arcadia, CA · New York). So I tried teaching instead. I taught in my hometown of Arcadia, California, then moved to New York with Teach For America, slated to teach history. After summer school I realized it wasn’t for me. I missed building, so I went back to startups.
  7. Thatch Health (New York · 2024–2025). I came back through Thatch Health, a health benefits startup in New York. On a four-person carrier ops team, we built the processes for enrollments and payments across more than 60 insurance carriers. During open enrollment I did the data work, building the dashboards we used to process about 2,000 enrollments.
  8. Conduit Financial (New York · 2025–now). Now I’m a Product Manager at Conduit Financial, back in fintech and into crypto. We built a multi-bank and stablecoin USD money-movement API for cross-border payments from zero to one in four months, and I’ve shipped 86 pull requests of production code myself.

Now
